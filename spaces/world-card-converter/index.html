Compatibility and privacy

  • Tested with SillyTavern Lorebooks whose entries field is an object or array.
  • Supports Characters, Locations, Organizations, Events, Rules and Items.
  • Disabled, empty or invalid entries are reported; unknown categories use your fallback type.
  • Source field names not represented by the tested mapping are listed before download.
  • The downloadable report records mapped, approximated, skipped and unsupported fields for review.
  • The download is a normalized structural fixture, not an official native CrushOn export.
