# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, give constructive feedback, and do not publish another person's private information. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized harassment, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact channel listed on the CrushOn.AI website. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
